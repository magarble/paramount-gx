# Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Browser Fix for Paramount+ does not collect, store, sell or transmit personal data, browsing history, account details, cookies or viewing activity to its developer or any analytics service. It contains no analytics, advertising or remote code.

The extension works locally. On Paramount+ pages, it changes browser identity values exposed to page scripts. For requests to paramountplus.com, it replaces browser identity headers. Paramount+ continues to receive the requests you make to its service under its own privacy policy.

## Permissions

- `https://*.paramountplus.com/*`: allows the extension to apply its compatibility changes to Paramount+ pages and requests.
- `declarativeNetRequestWithHostAccess`: lets the browser apply local rules replacing User-Agent and client-hint headers for the permitted domain. The extension does not read request or response bodies.

No additional permissions, accounts or registration are needed by the extension.

## Support

Report problems at https://github.com/magarble/paramount-gx/issues. GitHub handles information you choose to include in a report under its own privacy policy. Do not post passwords, cookies, payment details or account tokens.
